The details.

Size
About 147 × 71 × 8 mm with a 6.1-inch display. Small enough for one hand and a pocket, big enough to type on.
Shape
Round-faced body with wave-contoured sides and curves matched to the hand.
Body
600D aerospace-grade aramid fiber (Kevlar) with a tactile matte polyurethane coating.
Screen protection
Built-in UV-applied liquid tempered glass with round edges.
Cooling
Multi-layer graphite sheets and advanced thermal gels carry heat away from the chip and battery.
Port
Charging port with an integrated rubber dust plug.
Colours
Easter pastels: lavender, mint and other softly vibrant shades.
Personalisation
A shortcut interface for your own shortcuts, plus upgradable memory and storage.

Repairable and customizable.

ERGO is built to be opened, fixed and upgraded, so one phone can last for years.

  • Repairable by design. Replace a worn part instead of the whole phone.
  • Customizable memory. Upgrade the memory as your needs grow.
  • Customizable hard-drive space. Add storage so photos and music never force a new phone.

Sourced from verified supply chains.

Every component is traced from the mine to the finished phone.

  • Traceable components. Materials come only from verified supply chains.
  • Fairtrade Certified mines. All materials are sourced from mines that are Fairtrade Certified.
  • Third-party verified labour codes. Safety, working hours and more are independently checked.
